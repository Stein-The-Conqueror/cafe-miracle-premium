FROM TURKEY TO BEECHWOOD

A personal journey.
A place to belong.

In a local neighbourhood profile, owner Özlem Aslan described moving from Turkey to Canada with her family and finding meaning in building a business close to home. Her love of small rituals, flowers, and connecting with neighbours helped shape the café’s atmosphere.

The result is a place where Turkish coffee and Mediterranean-inspired food sit alongside a wider invitation: slow down, share a table, and make room for each other.

THE MIRACLE JAR & MIRACLE WALL

A small reminder.
A thousand voices.

The café’s Miracle Jar holds notes that invite guests to pause and see things with a little more kindness. Nearby, the Miracle Wall gathers words, drawings, wishes, and hopes shared by the community.

This digital wall is a simple, local-only demonstration of the idea. Notes you add here appear only in this browser session; they are not sent to the café or saved to a server.
