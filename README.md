# Café Miracle — two website concepts

These are two distinct, responsive website previews designed around publicly available information about Café Miracle at 141 Beechwood Ave., Ottawa.

## Start here

- **C$800-style concept:** open `starter/index.html`.
- **C$1,500-style concept:** open `premium/index.html`.

The pages are static HTML/CSS/JavaScript, so there is no build step, account, database, or package installation. For the full experience, unzip the folder and keep its directory structure intact. Opening the files in a browser should work; running a tiny local server is more reliable for browser clipboard features. Images and Google Fonts load from external hosts, so an internet connection is needed for the intended appearance.

## Concept A — C$800 single-page website

A carefully art-directed one-page home for the café:

- Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Clear first impression, location and call-to-action, menu highlights, story, COSMOS, and visit details.
- Direct phone, directions, Instagram, and in-page navigation links.
- Interactive “Miracle Jar” note generator inspired by the café’s real community tradition.
- Accessibility touches: skip link, focus states, semantic sections, alternative text, and reduced-motion support.
- No invented menu prices, online-ordering flow, or fake customer testimonials.

**Best fit:** a clean first website that gives visitors the essentials in a single scroll and points them to the café for current updates.

## Concept B — C$1,500 multi-page premium website

A broader brand experience with five connected pages:

- `premium/index.html` — home, highlights, brand story preview, digital Miracle Jar, and key calls to action.
- `premium/menu.html` — menu-highlight cards with functional category filters.
- `premium/cosmos.html` — the creative community space, workshop formats, and an Instagram/call contact route.
- `premium/story.html` — owner and café story, plus an interactive local-only Miracle Wall note demo.
- `premium/visit.html` — address, phone, current-updates link, directions, illustrative route graphic, and a copy-address interaction.
- Shared visual system, active-page navigation, responsive navigation, animation, and accessibility/reduced-motion considerations.

**Best fit:** a fuller online home that presents the café as both a neighbourhood coffee shop and a place to connect and create, and makes its unique community features easier to discover.

## Research notes and details to verify before launch

Research gathered on 13 October 2026. Key sources:

- Google Maps details supplied in the user's message: 4.8 rating, 156 reviews, 141 Beechwood Ave., and (613) 663-2233.
- Manor Park Chronicle, “Business Buzz,” café profile and later business brief: https://manorparkchronicle.com/business-buzz/
- Manor Park Chronicle advertiser directory: https://manorparkchronicle.com/advertising/directory-of-advertisers/
- Public listing: https://connectottawa.ca/coffee-shops/vanier/caf%C3%A9-miracle
- Café Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/beechwoodmiracle/

Public sources describe Café Miracle as a neighbourhood café founded by Özlem Aslan, shaped by Turkish heritage and Mediterranean-inspired food, with Turkish coffee called “Miracle Dust,” a community “Miracle Jar” and “Miracle Wall,” and a creative workshop space called COSMOS. Publicly mentioned daytime items include Spinel (spinach pastry with yogurt dip), Dilly (dill-feta scone), ChocoHug, Miracle BLT, Miracle Rings, and the Tofu Tango and Vega wraps. Visitor feedback also mentions coffee drinks, matcha, pastries, soup, sandwiches, and London Fog.

**Important verification items:** public directories display conflicting opening schedules, so the demos point to Instagram or the café by phone rather than publishing a fixed daily schedule. Menu highlights are not represented as a complete or guaranteed current menu; prices and availability are intentionally omitted. Confirm current menu, ingredient/allergen descriptions, event schedule, and business details directly with Café Miracle before production.

## Photo and production notes

- The storefront image is served from Connect Ottawa's public listing image URL. Confirm permission/usage terms or replace it with an owner-supplied image before launching commercially.
- Other café, food, and workshop imagery is illustrative remote photography from Unsplash URLs, not represented as photos of the actual premises or menu items. Replace with owner-approved, correctly licensed photos for a final client build.
- Google Fonts and external images require an internet connection.
- The digital Miracle Jar and Miracle Wall are demo interactions. Miracle Wall notes remain in the current page only, are not transmitted or saved, and disappear when the page reloads.
- No e-commerce, online ordering, payment, live booking, email dispatch, analytics, or CMS is connected. Those would need separate setup, approvals, and ongoing service choices.

## Preview deployment

For an in-person pitch, open the selected `index.html` on the laptop, navigate through the live demo, then show the `README.md` scope page. If deploying publicly, upload the corresponding folder to a static host after confirming the café's information, rights to imagery, final text, and owner approval.
